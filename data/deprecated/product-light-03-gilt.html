Delivered as an instant download link at checkout and by email.

Catchphrases stick. This workbook puts that stickiness to work — twelve themes of modern mantras, enough for a year, each one paired with prompts that turn a borrowed phrase into something that is actually yours. Follow the path start to finish, take one theme a month, or open to a page at random and let the fates decide.

What’s inside

  • Twelve overarching themes, groupable by month
  • A full-page illustrated mantra facing every worksheet
  • A weekly recall tracker on each page
  • Blank pages at the end for your own mantras
  • A grounding practice to begin from

The four prompts on every page

  1. How do I interpret this mantra?
  2. How can I relate this to my life?
  3. When can this be useful to me?
  4. What things may help me recall this mantra?
