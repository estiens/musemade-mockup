The four prompts on every page

  1. How do I interpret this mantra?
  2. How can I relate this to my life?
  3. When can this be useful to me?
  4. What things may help me recall this mantra?

How a spread works

Every mantra gets a full illustrated page, and the page facing it is yours to fill in.

Four prompts move the phrase from something you read to something you use — what it means, where it lands in your life, when to reach for it, and what will bring it back to mind.

A row of boxes at the foot of each page tracks how often you recalled it that week.
