Muse Made Coaching — design directions

Six clickable mockups in three directions. Each direction has a dark landing page and a light shop + workbook page that go together. Everything is self-contained — fonts and images are embedded, so these work offline and on a phone.

Main site — dark

Direction A

Ember

Warm charcoal, poster-scale condensed type, the workbook's retro sun as the signature graphic. Loud, confident, a little bit gig-poster.

 Direction B

Cut

Deep teal-black, split-screen hero, hairline rules and a numbered index. Sharper and more editorial — closer to a studio than a poster.
Direction C

Gilt

Her current site, done properly. Same Cinzel and Cormorant Garamond, same warm black and gold — centered, contact-forward, with the contrast raised and the tracking tightened so the small text is actually readable.
The real question

Two brands, one business

The live site is classical serif and gold. The workbook is retro condensed sans and primary color. Directions A and B pull the site toward the workbook; C pulls the workbook toward the site. That choice is the actual decision on the table.

 Light B — pairs with Cut

Shop & workbook page

Light C — pairs with Gilt

Shop & workbook page

Her cream, her serifs, her gold — laid out like a fine-press catalog, with the workbook's own color blocks doing the work the photography would. The lighter product feel she asked for, without leaving her existing brand.
How to use these

Mix and match

The pairings are suggestions, not packages. A dark landing from one direction and a light shop from another can work if the type system is shared — worth trying once she reacts to the six.

What's real and what's placeholder

  • Landing pages: every word is taken verbatim from the current live site — the tagline, "Welcome to your most satisfied self," the inquiry form and its dropdown options, the confirmation message, the phone and email. Nothing is invented.
  • Shop and product pages: copy and prices are placeholders. The structure is real; the words are there to be replaced.
  • Workbook images are actual spreads from Modern Mantras.
  • The forms and Add to Cart buttons are mockups — the inquiry form does show its "Message Received" state if you submit it.

Type & color

  • Ember: Anton for display, Poppins for body. Palette pulled from the workbook — cream #F4EFE2, ink #171310, orange #E96B2B, yellow #F5CC27, red #C72E2E.
  • Cut: Oswald for display, Inter for body. Sage #8FAFAE, deep teal #3B6560, salmon #F6A28A on the same cream and ink.
  • Gilt: Cinzel and Cormorant Garamond — pulled directly off the live site, along with warm black #1A1410, cream #F5F0E8 and gold #D4B87A / #B89A5C. On the light page the gold drops to #7E6329 for anything carrying text.
  • Every text/background pair on all four pages meets WCAG AA contrast. On the light pages the orange is darkened to #A2470F anywhere it carries text, because full-strength orange on cream does not pass.
Keep all seven files in the same folder so the links work. Fonts and images are embedded — no internet needed.
