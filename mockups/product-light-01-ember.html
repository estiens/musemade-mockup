Catchphrases stick. This workbook puts that stickiness to work — twelve themes of modern mantras, enough for a year, each one paired with prompts that turn a borrowed phrase into something that is actually yours.

Follow the path start to finish, take one theme a month, or open to a page at random and let the fates decide. Blank pages at the back are there for the mantras you write yourself.
